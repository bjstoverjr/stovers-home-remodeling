FAQ

Common questions

Are estimates really free?

Yes. On-site estimates for repairs and remodels are free, with no obligation.

Do you work with my insurance company?

We work with homeowners on insurance-related repairs every day. We document the damage, prepare a detailed estimate and can meet your adjuster on-site to review the scope. We're a contractor, not a public adjuster, so we don't negotiate or settle your claim. That stays between you and your insurer.

Will you pay or waive my deductible?

No. Paying, waiving or rebating an insurance deductible isn't something a reputable contractor offers, and Missouri law prohibits it for roof and exterior work. Be wary of anyone who does.

What areas do you serve?

The Greater Kansas City metro, up to about 50 miles from downtown, on both the Missouri and Kansas sides. We're based in Blue Springs.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. Repairs and punch-list work are welcome, along with full remodels and commercial buildouts.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're licensed, bonded and insured. Ask us for license details and proof of insurance for your city or county anytime.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job. Call or send a request and we'll give you a realistic start date with your estimate.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. We handle commercial buildouts and repairs for local businesses and property managers.
