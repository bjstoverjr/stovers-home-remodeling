Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Stover's Home Remodeling ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through stoversremodeling.com and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you send us: when you request an estimate, we collect your name, phone number, email address (optional), ZIP code, the type of work you need, whether it involves an insurance claim, and any details you include.
  • When you call, text or email us: your phone number or email address and what you tell us.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard information such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, prepare estimates, schedule and complete work, and keep records of our jobs. Requests may be stored in our email and in the job-management software we use to run our business.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may call or text you about your request or project. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts or HELP for help. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with anyone for their marketing.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (such as our website host, email and job-management software), with your insurance company or adjuster when you ask us to, or when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If we add tools such as Google Analytics in the future, we will update this policy to describe them and how to opt out.
